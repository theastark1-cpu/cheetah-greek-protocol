Common Questions

Q: How do I titrate Reta from 1mg to 2mg safely?

Don't jump. You've done 1mg (10u), now go to 1.5mg (30u) for 2 weeks, THEN 2mg (40u). 100% dose jumps cause severe nausea. Slow titration matters. Run at 2mg for 4 weeks max, then OFF.

Q: Why such a high dose of IGF-1 (10mg vial)?

Standard is 1mg, but your vial is 10× larger. Critical: 10mg vial needs 20mL BAC to get the correct 500mcg/mL concentration. At standard 2mL BAC, you'd be drawing 0.8 units (impossible). With 20mL, 40mcg = 8u — manageable and accurate. Vial lasts 250 doses at 40mcg.

Q: Why such a high dose of GHK-Cu (100mg vial)?

100mg at 10mL BAC = 10mg/mL = 1mg = 10u. Standard protocol. The 100mg vial just lasts 100+ doses = 3+ months. More cost-effective long-term, single reconstitution.

Q: Can I combine SS-31 with other peptides?

Yes — BPC-157, KPV, and Ipa are pH-compatible with SS-31. Combined syringe = 1 poke instead of 3. NEVER combine with GHK-Cu (copper precipitates SS-31). Inject SS-31 combo at different site than GHK-Cu solo.

Q: Why no Tesamorelin in this stack?

You're running Reta 2mg for fat loss. Tesa's role (VAT reduction) is now covered by Reta (which is more powerful for fat loss). Ipamorelin alone provides the GH pulse amplification without Tesa's histamine issue. This stack is leaner and more focused.
