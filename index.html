Common Questions

Q: How do I titrate Reta from 1mg to 2mg safely?

Don't jump. You've done 1mg (10u), now go to 1.5mg (30u) for 2 weeks, THEN 2mg (40u). 100% dose jumps cause severe nausea. Slow titration matters. Run at 2mg for 4 weeks max, then OFF.

Q: Why such a high dose of IGF-1 (10mg vial)?

Standard is 1mg, but your vial is 10× larger. Critical: 10mg vial needs 20mL BAC to get the correct 500mcg/mL concentration. At standard 2mL BAC, you'd be drawing 0.8 units (impossible). With 20mL, 40mcg = 8u — manageable and accurate. Vial lasts 250 doses at 40mcg.

Q: Why such a high dose of GHK-Cu (100mg vial)?

100mg at 3mL BAC = 33.3 mg/mL = 1mg = 3u (very small, accurate draw). The 100mg vial lasts 100 doses = 3+ months. More cost-effective long-term, single reconstitution. Note: 33mg/mL is highly concentrated — verify your source's recommended max concentration (some cap at 10mg/mL).

Q: How often should I take TB-500?

Mon+Thu only (2×/week) — 5 mg (100u) aggressive for 8-week joint protocol. Then taper to 2.5 mg (50u) 1×/week × 2 wks, then OFF 4+ wks. Standard dose (post-protocol): 2.5 mg Mon+Thu loading 4 wks → 1×/week maintenance 4–8 wks → OFF 2–4 wks. TB-500 has a 48–72h systemic half-life — Mon+Thu = no overlap, sustained exposure. Don't dose daily. A 10mg vial at 2mL BAC gives 4 doses at 5 mg or 8 doses at 2.5 mg.

Q: Why are BPC-157 and TB-500 doses escalated? Is it safe?

🔧 Aggressive 8-week joint protocol for active knee/shoulder pain. BPC escalation to 1 mg/day (500 mcg AM systemic + 500 mcg periarticular PM local) is supported by Sikiric et al. rodent data — no safety signal at multiples of the standard 500 mcg/day dose. TB-500 escalation to 5 mg 2×/week is more aggressive — based on equine dose translation; human data is anecdotal but not harmful. This is a finite 8-week tool, not a permanent dose. After 8 wks: BPC back to 500 mcg/day PM only, TB-500 back to 50u maintenance. Vial cost: ~$240–360 for full 8-week supply.

Q: How do I inject BPC-157 periarticular without hitting the joint?

Stay 1 inch from joint line, inject into soft tissue (subcutaneous fat, muscle belly). For knees: around patella (above, below, sides). For shoulders: subacromial space, around rotator cuff. Rotate sites daily — don't repeatedly inject the same spot (lipoatrophy risk). If you hit the joint capsule itself (intra-articular), it requires imaging guidance and different risk profile — avoid.

Q: Can I combine SS-31 with other peptides?

SS-31 paused this round. When you resume: Yes — BPC-157, KPV, and Ipa are pH-compatible with SS-31. NEVER combine with GHK-Cu (copper precipitates SS-31). Inject SS-31 combo at different site than GHK-Cu solo.
